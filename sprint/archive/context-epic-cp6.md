THE QUARRY EXISTS, AND CENTIPEDE IS TEMPEST'S LINEAGE, NOT JOUST'S. This is the single most important fact for scoping: joust had to SYNTHESISE stand-ins because its sound board firmware was never vendored (JOUSTSND.DOC is a three-line pointer to a file no revision carries). centipede has the real thing. reference/atari-source/centipede/revision.v4/CENTI4.MAC:2322-2465 is a complete POKEY sound engine — the SOUNDS routine writing AUDF0-3 and AUDC0-3 directly, plus SIX data tables carrying the machine's own comments: FREQ0/CONT0 :2455-2456 ';EXPLOSION SOUND', FREQ1/CONT1 :2457-2458 ';SOUND OF LITTLE FEET' and ';MUST BE REPEATED', FREQ2 :2459 ';SHOT SOUND', FREQ3/CONT3 :2461-2462 ';WELL REPEAT UNTIL TURNED OFF', FREQ4 :2463-2464, and FREQ6 :2465. So samples are BAKED FROM DATA here, the way plugins/tempest/tools/pokey-bake/bake-sfx.mjs does it, and a synthesised stand-in is a fallback that needs a written reason, not the default. THE MANIFEST PROMISES MORE THAN THE ROM HAS, and cp6-1 exists to rule on it rather than let a baker guess. The ROM drives SEVEN channel countdown variables over FOUR POKEY voices, allocated by its own header comment at :2325-2328 — CHAN0 all explosions, CHAN1 bonus/centipede/ant/scorpion, CHAN2 shot, CHAN3 spider. The writers are CHAN0 :2300 ';EXPLOSION SOUND', CHAN1 :1289 ';START LITTLE FEET NOISE', CHAN2 :2134 ';SHOT SOUND', CHAN3 :431 on with :282 and :2255 off, CHAN4 :1995 ';BONUS LIFE SOUND', CHAN5 :1812 ';EXPLOSION SOUND FOR PLAYER' and CHAN6 :2025 on with :155 ';NO SCORPION SOUND'. There is NO FREQ5: the player explosion replays FREQ0/CONT0 with ADC I,02 ';INCREASE VOLUME' at :2444-2449, so it is the general explosion made louder rather than a sound of its own. Six tables and one derived variant against fourteen declared cues means several manifest entries have no table behind them; which collapse onto the shared explosion and which are honest inventions is a RULING, and an unruled baker will fabricate authenticity. RADIX IS LOAD-BEARING AND HAS BITTEN THIS REPO BEFORE (the rb4-1 lesson, restated in cp1 and cp4). CENTI4.MAC inherits .RADIX 16 from CENDE4 via .INCLUDE, so every bare literal in those tables is HEX unless it carries a trailing period — FREQ0's 0F0,0E0,0D0 are hex, 'LDA I,14' at the CHAN3 reset is hex 14 (decimal 20), and 'LDY I,20.' in the scorpion's continuous-loop block is decimal 20. A baker that reads those bytes as decimal produces confidently wrong tones that no test can see. Every transcribed constant carries a radix-cited comment and a claims entry, and the citation gate is live for this game. TWO THINGS EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE.
