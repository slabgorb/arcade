## Problem

The last thing the df3 ship needs is a gun. Defender's laser is a short burst of
fast bolts fired in the direction the ship faces, capped at a handful on screen at
once. It is a scheduler process like everything else. `df3` gives it firing and
travel; what it *hits* (enemies) is `df4`, so this story deliberately stops at the
bolt leaving the screen.

## Technical Approach

`src/core/laser.ts` — a pure laser, spawned as a scheduler (super)process off df3-1.

- **Fire, capped.** `LFIRE` (`defender/DEFA7.SRC:2763-2766`) refuses when `LFLG`
  (`defender/PHR6.SRC:302`) has reached its cap (`LFLG >= 4` in the ROM path) and
  otherwise increments it and spawns a bolt. The cap is the "max concurrent lasers".
- **Direction by facing.** A positive `NPLAD` fires **right** (`LASR`,
  `defender/DEFA7.SRC:2790`); negative fires **left** (`LASL`,
  `defender/DEFA7.SRC:2839`) — the ship's current facing (df3-3) picks the branch.
  The bolt spawns at the ship's muzzle offset (`LEAX $704,X`, `defender/DEFA7.SRC:2792`).
- **Travel + death.** The bolt travels fast in its direction each tick and dies when
  it runs off-screen (releasing its `LFLG` slot). The laser record fields are
  `LFLG`/`LCOLRX` (`defender/PHR6.SRC:301-303`).
- **No enemy collision.** `df3` fires and travels only. `LCOL`/`COLIDE`
  (`defender/DEFA7.SRC:2775-2788`) against enemies is **df4** — do not wire it here
  (there are no enemies yet).

## Scope

- **In scope:** fire (capped by `LFLG`), left/right spawn by facing, muzzle offset,
  bolt travel, off-screen death releasing the slot.
- **Out of scope:** laser↔enemy collision (df4), laser sound (df6 — the `LASSND`
  command at `defender/DEFA7.SRC:686` is noted, not synthesized), the exact bolt
  colour cycling beyond an index.
