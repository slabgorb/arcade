# Story sw11-1 Context

## Title
Trench return-fire is grossly too lethal — port the ROM's THREE base-gun mechanisms the sw10 basis unification newly exposed. Every difficulty CONSTANT is already faithful; these are MECHANISM omissions (cf. asteroids-rom-retune). Our stepTrench fires every 'turret' within TRENCH_GUN_FIRE_RANGE (0x6000) each opening on a flat `nextInt(rng,256) > threshold` roll, spawns a PERFECT analytic lead (trenchGunFireVelocity), and caps concurrent shots at MAX_FIREBALL_SLOTS (6). The cabinet does all three very differently. (1) AIM — biggest lever. The ROM never leads: PANLIN (WSGUNS.MAC:409-432) spawns the shell at the gun's OWN panel position with a semi-random Z angle (`LDA P.RND1 / LSRA ;45 DEGREES`), top-panel guns level; then MOVPL/MOVPR (WSGUNS.MAC:788-846) move it with a heavily DAMPED, DIRECTIONALLY-LIMITED heat-seek — Z drifts toward the player at ASRD4 (>>4 = 1/16 of the gap per frame); Y only heat-seeks the player from WV.HRD>=1 (`CMPA #1 / IFHS ... LDD M$TY ;HEAT SEEK`), and below that just drifts to +/-180 (channel centre), and a LEFT-wall shell may ONLY move right, a RIGHT-wall shell ONLY left. Net: authentic shells scatter and usually miss; ours never do. Replace the closed-form lead with a random-angle spawn + damped per-frame heat-seek matching MOVPL/MOVPR. (2) CONCURRENCY — GNBSAVAIL (WSGUNS.MAC:210-244) caps base-gun shells by the per-difficulty TGNBS table `GUNZ-<N*G$IZE>` = N airborne, N = [1,1,2,2,3,3,3,4] over WV.HRD 0..7. Wave-1 trench (WV.HRD 0) allows just ONE base shell in the air; our flat 6 is 6x. Cap concurrent base shots at TGNBS[gunDiff], not MAX_FIREBALL_SLOTS. (`GNAVAIL`/'FIRE EVERYTHING' is the port-in-view end-run only.) (3) ELIGIBILITY — BSGUN (WSBASE.MAC:1236-1341) fires a gun only when the player is ABOVE the bunker (`SUBD M.Z0 / IFGE ?PLAYER ABOVE BUNKER?`) AND within the near height band (`SUBD #400 / IFLT`) at full prob, OR within one lower band at the SQUARED prob `P.RND1 * P.RND1` (two uniforms => skewed low); never below, and never point-blank (`ADDA #20+4 ;(DONT SHOOT IF TOO CLOSE)`). #400 is one wall-panel height (Z walks -0200 down by #400 x4); map to our WALL_SLOT_Y spacing. Port the vertical-alignment gate + squared far-branch + too-close exclusion into the stepTrench gun loop. Pin all three with deterministic tests (fixed seed + fixed player height: assert only the aligned subset fires, a point-blank gun never fires, concurrent base shots <= TGNBS[gunDiff], and a spawned shell's angle is the random/level value not a lead). Verify playability by hand on the dev server.
