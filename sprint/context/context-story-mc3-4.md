Grow GameState to add icbms, cities, bases, score, phase, remaining (per-wave budget), and a seeded rng (Rng from @shared/rng). createGame(seed=1) seeds 6 live cities, 3 live bases at full ammo, empty enemy list, phase 'play', remaining=NICBMS. stepGame runs the 7-step per-frame order: (1) spawn against live targets, (2) fly ICBMs, (3) fly ABMs, (4) detonate ABM arrivals into blasts, (5) damage — blasts kill ICBMs (scored) and arrived ICBMs kill structures, (6) age blasts, (7) resolve score + phase. When phase='over' the loop only advances frame. Consumes every module from mc3-1/2/3 plus mc1's abm/explosion/cursor. Depends on mc3-1, mc3-2, mc3-3.

- createGame(seed) returns a fresh game: 6 live cities, 3 live bases each with ammo 10, no ICBMs, score 0, phase 'play'.
