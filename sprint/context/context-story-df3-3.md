## Problem

With the kernel (df3-1) and the world/camera model (df3-2) in place, the ship
becomes a real actor: a scheduler process the player flies with thrust, reverse and
vertical control. The feel of that flight lives in details the ROM is specific
about — a **24-bit** X velocity accumulator (sub-pixel), a facing flip on reverse
with a switch debounce, and a vertical axis the player is clamped inside. Getting
any of these approximate makes the ship feel wrong slowly.

## Technical Approach

`src/core/ship.ts` — a pure player process (created via df3-1's `makeProcess`),
driven each tick by a **pure input snapshot** the shell hands in (thrust / reverse /
up / down / fire; the ROM reads these from the PIA, `defender/DEFA7.SRC:2360,2443`).

- **Sub-pixel X velocity — 24-bit.** `PLAXV RMB 3` (`defender/PHR6.SRC:332`) is
  **three bytes**. Thrust adds to `PLAXV` in the facing direction (accel at
  `defender/DEFA7.SRC:2360-2371`); the low byte (`PLAXV+2`) is the sub-pixel
  fraction. **Do not truncate to 16 bits** — the acceleration curve depends on the
  carry out of the fraction.
- **Integration.** `VELO` (`defender/DEFA7.SRC:2480-2499`) walks the object list
  adding `OXV`→`OX16` and `OYV`→`OY16` each tick; the player's on-screen X/Y
  (`PLAX16`/`PLAY16`) advance the same way, feeding df3-2's slide. Velocity clamps
  to `±$0100` (`defender/DEFA7.SRC:2421-2428`).
- **Reverse.** `REV` (`defender/DEFA7.SRC:3155-3171`) negates `PLADIR` into `NPLAD`
  (flip facing) behind a `REVFLG` debounce (`defender/PHR6.SRC:295`) so one press is
  one flip.
- **Vertical.** `PLAUP`/`PLADN` (`defender/DEFA7.SRC:2441-2476`) move `PLAY16` up/down
  and **clamp** to `[YMIN+1,238]` (df3-2's player rule) — the ship cannot wrap
  vertically the way objects do.

## Scope

- **In scope:** the ship process — thrust (24-bit accel), reverse (facing flip +
  debounce), vertical (clamped), integrated via `VELO`, consuming df3-2's world.
- **Out of scope:** the laser (df3-3 does not fire — that's df3-5); stars (df3-4);
  collision against enemies (df4); hyperspace/smart-bomb (df5, and they strobe — out).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `plugins/defender/src/core/ship.ts` exists: a pure ship process created via the
      df3-1 kernel, stepped each tick from a **pure input snapshot** (no PIA/DOM read
      in core).
- [ ] X velocity is a **24-bit** accumulator (`PLAXV`, incl. `PLAXV+2`); a test proves
      sub-pixel accumulation across ticks is retained (not truncated to 16 bits).
- [ ] Thrust accelerates in the facing direction and velocity clamps at `±$0100`
      (`defender/DEFA7.SRC:2421-2428`); a mutant on the clamp reddens.
- [ ] Reverse flips `PLADIR` facing exactly once per press (the `REVFLG` debounce,
      `defender/DEFA7.SRC:3157-3159`) and the new facing changes the df3-2 ship-leads
      target column (`$20`↔`$70`).
- [ ] Vertical motion **clamps** the player to `[YMIN+1,238]` (`defender/DEFA7.SRC:2450,2461`)
      — the ship does not wrap vertically.
- [ ] Every constant enrolls in `claims/*.json`, byte-verified by `citations.test.ts`;
      `purity.test.ts` green; ROM cites as `defender/<FILE>.SRC:<line>`.
