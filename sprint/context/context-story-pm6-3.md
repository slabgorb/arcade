## Technical Approach
The remaining two scenes, layered on the pm6-2 scripted-actor player — same file surface
(the cutscene module), distinct scripted animations.

- **Act 2 (ripped-ghost / "nail").** Blinky snags on a nail and his sheet tears — the classic
  torn-sheet scene. New scripted timeline on the pm6-2 player.
- **Act 3 (worm / tearing-ghost).** The tattered ghost / worm scene. Second scripted timeline.
- **Consume pm2/pm3.** Reuse the pm6-2 player and pm3 baked sprites; no new framework, no new
  bake.
- **RED-anchor (Decision C) + accessibility (Decision B).** Paths/timing pinned at RED to the
  source + dossier, cited; gentle animations, no strobe. A test pins the **act→level mapping**
  (which act plays at which level cadence). Every constant → `citations.test.ts`.
