## Technical Approach
Root story of epic pm4 (pm4-6/7/8/9/10 all `depend_on` pm4-5). Extend the core
`GamePhase` enum in `plugins/pac-man/src/core/game.ts` from `playing|game-over`
to add `attract|ready|dying|level-clear`, plus a **pure MAINLINE dispatch** over
**frame-count timers** — seeded, **no clock** (the mc6-1 contract; mirrors
missile-command mc6). The core-boundary/`purity.test.ts` scan of `src/core/`
MUST stay green: attract/ready/dying/clear timers are frame counts + seeded RNG,
never `Date`/`performance`/wall-clock.

State loop to implement:
- `attract` --(start/coin)--> `ready`
- `ready` --(ready timer)--> `playing`
- `playing` --(pac-died, lives>0)--> `dying` --(death timer)--> `ready`
- `playing` --(pac-died, lives=0)--> `game-over`
- `playing` --(all dots)--> `level-clear` --(clear timer)--> `ready` (level+1)
- `game-over` --(timeout)--> `attract`
