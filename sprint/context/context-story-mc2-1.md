## Problem
Port joust/centipede's citation-checker machinery onto missile-command and retrofit EVERY constant the mc1 skeleton hardcoded into an enforced claim. Today every citation in the skeleton (field.ts CITIES/BASES/NCITY/NMISBA, cursor.ts IHMIN/IHMAX/IVMIN/IVMAX/TOPSCR, explosion.ts EXDONE, abm.ts velocity, render.ts LOGICAL_HEIGHT) lives in a code COMMENT checked by nobody. This story turns those into machine-verified claims so mc3/mc4 write their large constant surface UNDER the guardrail rather than retrofitting it later.

REUSE-FIRST: copy the shapes from plugins/joust/tools/audit/check-citations.mjs + plugins/joust/tests/helpers/claims.ts (centipede carries the same). Establish plugins/missile-command/docs/rom-study/claims/*.json. Invent no new machinery.

THREE TRAPS the checker MUST survive (memory: mc-w3main-cite-double-spaced): (1) W3MAIN is DOUBLE-SPACED — resolve LOGICAL (non-blank) line numbers ~physical/2, cite via .SBTTL/symbol not physical offset; (2) .RADIX 16 at W3COMN.MAC:1 inherited everywhere — a bare number is HEX, a trailing '.' is DECIMAL (MAXMIS=10. vs CITY2H=0B4); (3) the vendored .MAC are CR-terminated non-UTF8 — grep -a or they read as binary false-empty.

Design: plugins/missile-command/docs/design/mc2-dossier.md. Ground truth: docs/rom-study/brief.md.

## Acceptance Criteria
