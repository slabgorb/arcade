It is Reviewer-driven and EXPECTED TO FILE STORIES, not just close them: each completed story buys one Reviewer mutation battery over one file surface, and a battery over a weakly-asserted surface reliably yields surviving mutants, each filed as its own story. GROOMING RULE (the jt9 gotcha): group the backlog by the FILES each story edits, NOT by subsystem or the ROM routine it cites — two stories touching the same guard/test-file/decoder are one story with two commits; two stories citing adjacent ROM lines but editing different files are two stories and merging them buys nothing but a bigger diff. A mutant that reddens nothing is a finding (zero mutation coverage), not redundancy — trace reachability first. The seed stories below are a starting battery over the highest-risk, weakest-asserted surfaces (the CONWAY Life field, the scroll geometry, the two-POKEY driver); the epic grows as batteries surface mutants.
