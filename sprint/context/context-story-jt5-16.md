THE ROM MAKES THE PTERODACTYL A FULL COLLISION PARTICIPANT. The no-kill branch is taken at ':4961 BNE OSTHT2  BR=NO KILL (ENEMY VS. ENEMY, PTERO VS. PTERO)'. OSTHT2 then sounds the cue BEFORE it knows who collided — ':5019 OSTHT2 LDX #SNETHD  ENEMIES COLIDE' / ':5020 JSR VSND' — and only afterwards tests PID for the pterodactyl id on BOTH parties (':5022-5027', CMPA #$80+PTEID, BEQ OSTH12 / BEQ OSTH13). A ptero-vs-BIRD pair goes to PTEBRD (':5034', and ':5037-5038' after 'EXG X,U' so the ptero is in U, per the ROM's own ':5036 * REG.U IS PTERODACTYL'). A ptero-vs-PTERO pair falls back through ':5031-5033 OSTH12 ... BEQ OSTH11  BR=YES, SAME OLD COLISION' to ':5028 OSTH11 JSR OSTBMP  NO-ONE DIES, BUT BUMP EACH OTHER ANYWAYS' — the ordinary bump, with SNETHD already playing.

THE GAP IN THIS PORT. collisionPass's pair loop filters to kind 'player' or 'enemy' (plugins/joust/src/core/demo.ts, the 'const eligible = processes.filter' line), and a ptero is resolved only through resolvePteroAttack against a PLAYER — so two pteros never meet. jt3-5's baiters cap at three live (MAX_BAITERS, src/core/baiter.ts) and a wave carries its own pteros besides, so pairs really do occur in ordinary play: the cue is reachable in principle and unreachable in fact.

WHAT IS ALREADY DONE, so this story does not redo it. The 'enemy-thud' event kind EXISTS in src/core/events.ts and its comment already names ptero-vs-ptero; jt5-4 wired SNETHD (:8106, priority 009) through the audio seam. NOTHING new is needed on the cue side. The whole of this story is reachability: admit pteros to the eligible set, route a ptero/ptero pair to the ordinary bump and emit enemy-thud, and leave ptero-vs-bird on its existing resolvePteroAttack path.
