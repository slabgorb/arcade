## Problem

The starfield is what makes the scroll legible — a parallax layer that drifts with
the camera and sells the sense of a moving world before any enemy exists. It reads
its motion straight off df3-2's camera delta. The one hazard is transcription:
`STOUT` is **self-modifying code**, so a literal opcode port is meaningless — the
star index stores are patched at runtime and the source flags them with `BSO BONER`
comments.

## Technical Approach

`src/core/stars.ts` — a pure star layer, composited into `df2`'s `framebuffer.ts`
by palette **index** (never a colour hex).

- **Motion from the camera delta.** `STOUT` (`defender/DEFA7.SRC:2095-2155`) takes
  the `BGL`−`BGLX` delta (masked, `defender/DEFA7.SRC:2101-2108`) as the star
  movement for the frame — the same camera df3-2 owns. Stars scroll opposite the
  world at a fixed rate (parallax).
- **Edge wrap.** A star crossing the screen edge re-enters the other side
  (`defender/DEFA7.SRC:2142-2149`, the `$9C`/`$C0` overflow check).
- **Active count.** `STRCNT` (`defender/PHR6.SRC:294`) is how many stars are live;
  it is cut down during some effects (`defender/DEFA7.SRC:3065`) — model the count,
  not the effect (that's later).
- **Phase mask.** `STOUT` derives a phase/colour mask from `BGL` bits
  (`defender/DEFA7.SRC:2117-2121`) — port the resulting per-star colour **index**.
- **The SMC trap.** The indexed stores at `defender/DEFA7.SRC:2151-2153` (and the
  `SBLNK` twins at `:2187-2189`) are `FCB`-byte-patched `STA [SX,X]` instructions —
  the `BSO BONER` comments mark them. Transcribe the **behaviour** ("store this
  star's colour into its cell") in clean TS; do **not** reproduce the opcode-patch.
  Record an `encoding`/behaviour note (streams-are-not-rasters cousin).

## Scope

- **In scope:** the pure star layer — positions, camera-delta scroll, edge wrap,
  active count, phase colour index, blit-by-index into the framebuffer.
- **Out of scope:** star *blink* random effects beyond a basic phase (keep minimal),
  hyperspace star scatter (df5), anything reading entropy in core.
