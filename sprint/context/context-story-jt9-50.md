## Problem
The Joust bounder's up-climb ignores the terrain above it. In the ROM, before a
bounder commits to climbing it first checks whether a cliff/platform blocks the cell
one step up, and if so it abandons the climb and flies level (`BOLEV`). The port's
bounder commits to the climb blind — it never samples the cell above. Found by the
jt9-23 Dev; distinct from the hunter/shadow climb paths (`B2UP3`/`SHUP3`) that jt9-23
already wired.

## ROM ground truth (SM-verified against the current tree)
Source: `reference/williams-source/joust/JOUSTRV4.SRC`. Verified verbatim at setup —
the citation is accurate, not stale.

```
3844 BOUNUP  CMPD  BOUPRG        #-DYLEN   LONG OR SHORT RANGE SEEK
3845         BGT   BOLEV          BR=SHORT RANGE SEEK
3846 BOUP    LDX   PPOSX,U       CLIFF IN THE WAY??
3847         LDY   PPOSY,U
3848         LDA   BCKXTB,X
3849         ANDA  BCKYTB-DYLEN,Y
3850         BNE   BOLEV          BR=YES        <- cliff above -> fly level
3851         LDD   BOUPDI        #DYLEN*256  NO, SET DISTANCE TO GO UP
3852         STD   PDIST,U
3853         BRA   BOUP1A                       <- clear -> commit to the climb
```

- `BOUP` (:3846) samples the background collision map **one `DYLEN` above** the
  bounder: `BCKXTB,X` indexed by column, AND'd with `BCKYTB-DYLEN,Y` indexed by row.
- On a **hit** (`BNE BOLEV`, :3850) the bounder diverts to plain level flight — it does
  NOT climb.
- On **no hit** it loads `BOUPDI` (`#DYLEN*256`) into `PDIST` and falls to `BOUP1A`,
  the actual up-climb.
- **"no BOUP3":** the bounder's blocked-climb has no separate up-climb subroutine —
  unlike the hunter (`B2UP3`) and shadow (`SHUP3`) that jt9-23 wired. This is the
  bounder-kind path only.

## Technical Approach
Bounder logic is pure simulation → lives in `plugins/joust/src/core/`. The change adds
the cliff-above sample to the bounder's up-climb decision: before committing to the
climb, test the background/terrain cell one step above; on a blocked cell, fly level
instead of climbing. Mirror the sampling the hunter/shadow paths use, but for the
bounder kind. Keep it deterministic and free of shell/DOM references (core boundary
scanner reads source text incl. comments — avoid `window.`/`document.`).
