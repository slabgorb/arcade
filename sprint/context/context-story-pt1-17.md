## Findings (research complete — verified against the vendored source)

**Our table** — `plugins/pac-man/src/core/level.ts:100-122` `FRUIT_PROGRESSION`:
cherry(1), strawberry(2), orange(3-4), apple(5-6), melon(7-8), galaxian(9-10),
bell(11-12), key(13+ via `KEY_FRUIT`, level.ts clamp). Naming note: our `orange` is
the user's "peach", our `melon` is "grapes" (the dossier's graphics claims use the
peach/grapes names — `docs/rom-study/claims/graphics.json:150,166`).

**The ROM encodes the double literally.** The level→fruit-kind table is the data
block at addr `3b08` — `pacman.asm:8739-8765` (the disassembler mis-renders it as
instructions; it is (sprite, colour) byte pairs indexed by level). `3b0c`/`3b0e`
hold `98 15` **twice** — orange/peach at levels 3 AND 4 — and every fruit L3-L12
repeats for two consecutive levels. Consumer routine: `pacman.asm:6075`
(`ld a,(#4e13)` level read), `:6077` (`cp #08`), `:6079` (`ld de,#3b08 ; Fruit
table?`), with the ≥8 sliding-window path at `:6116-6119`.
