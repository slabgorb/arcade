## Problem
Highest-impact defect from the 2026-08-20 render audit. composeFrame in scene.ts blits only state.landers and state.humanoids; the other banks (mutants, baiters/UFOs, bombers/TIEs, pods, swarmers, bombs) are live in SimState (sim.ts:212-217), scheduler-driven, and fully collidable (enemyObjects adds every bank, sim.ts:722-727) but drawn nowhere. They only blink in for one frame at spawn (materialize effect) and death (explosion); for their whole roaming/shooting life they are invisible. Collision already uses the same projection as the would-be render (toScreenCol is projectWorldX, sim.ts:626), so positions are correct — the defect is purely a missing blit loop.
Fix: add a composeFrame blit loop over the five (six with bombs) banks, projecting each through projectWorldX like landers. Sprite bytes for baiter (UFOP1) and swarmer (SWPIC1) are non-black and render immediately; mutant (SCZP1), bomber (TIEP1), pod (PRBP1) and bomb (BMBP1) use indices A-F and additionally need pt1-22's live palette to be visible.
Depends on pt1-22 for the TIE/pod/bomb/mutant colours; the blit loop itself can be built in parallel.
