## Technical Approach

**Measured Cabinet Behavior:** A TIE maintains a lateral offset (x≠0) and carries it across the field as it closes. TIE #2 and #3 steer toward x≈0 during approach, but never converge to pure centerline zoom.

**Implementation Area:** TIE choreography and flight model in star-wars/src/core (particularly TIE approach phase in gameRules.ts and flight simulation modules). The game logic tick is 20 Hz. **Test Surface:** Deterministic core simulation — trajectory probes in the TIE approach phase can measure x-position over time and verify lateral offset is maintained across field crossing, not collapsed to centerline.
