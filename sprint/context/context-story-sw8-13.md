## Problem
The cabinet has ONE pokey-music (PM) player: starting any tune or music kills
whatever is still playing. Concretely at the space→surface warp, the ground-init
towers music (PM4TH, WSMAIN.MAC:1636) STEALS the descent tune (PMDES) if it is
still ringing, and any new tune silences the music loop. Our shell (sw3-5
adaptation) runs TWO channels — `tune` + `music` — and overlays them, so after
sw8-12's milestone port the descent tune now rings ~1s INTO the towers music
across the warp: an overlap the cabinet cannot produce.

Filed by TEA during sw8-12 (see `sprint/archive/sw8-12-session.md`, Delivery
Findings + Impact Summary). sw8-12's deviation log explicitly assigns "the wider
channel-priority model" to this story.

## Technical Approach — RULED (TEA, RED phase)
**Single logical channel.** Every PM entry point — music (PMTH5 :372, PM4TH
:379, PMTHB :386, PMDAR :400, PMRRP :365) and tunes (PMSF2 :330 … PMDES :393)
alike — claims POKEY voices 1-4 via four `.TUNE` invocations, and the macro's
expansion opens with `JSR PKCUT'VNUM ;CLEAR OUT AND INIT THIS VOICE`
(SNDPM.MAC:319-326): an unconditional clear, no priority test, no resume. The
steal is SYMMETRIC — even the trench's repeating PMRRP dies to the torpedo's
PMSF2 — which refutes the narrower "music-starts-kill-tunes" option.

Port: unify the shell's `music` + `tune` logical channels into ONE (the shared
engine already steals per-channel, loop or one-shot — `@shared/audio`
startSource → stopChannel). No resume after a steal. SFX (SNDAUD effect
voices) and speech (TMS5220) are other hardware and stay outside the steal.
Also port the PHESP1 death gate: `LDA S.GAS / LBMI PHIS0D` (WSMAIN.MAC:
1396-1397) precedes the PH.TIM walk, so a milestone crossing on the frame the
LAST shield falls cues nothing (gate the sim.ts milestone block on post-hit
lives > 0). A tune ringing ACROSS gameover is ruled FAITHFUL — PHIS0D skips
only speech (`JSR SPKSKP`, :2036); PM voices ring until the endgame cues
(cantina/Ben) steal them.

RED tests: `tests/shell/pm-channel.test.ts` (new — union pin, symmetric-steal
behavior, no-resume, SFX/speech isolation), the flipped pin in
`tests/shell/tune-channel.test.ts`, and the death-frame describe in
`tests/core/space-music-milestones.test.ts`.
