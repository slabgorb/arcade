## Problem

implements this in MLSUB.MAC: `SCROLL` (~line 1105), `SCROLD` (~1149, scroll
down) and `SCROLU` (~1309, scroll up) shift the mushroom field vertically. The
clone's core must be scroll-aware from the start, or every later story that
reads playfield coordinates inherits a fixed-field assumption.
