## Technical Approach
Decision A: the intermission is a **phase off the pm4 machine**, pure-first.

- **Add the phase.** Extend `plugins/pac-man/src/core/game.ts:184` `GamePhase`
  (`attract|ready|playing|dying|level-clear|game-over`) with `intermission`, entered from the
  `level-cleared` transition (`events.ts:78`) at the ROM-cited level cadence, and returning to
  `ready` for the next level when it ends.
- **Gate on the level byte.** The ROM reads the level byte `#4e13` and compares it to decide
  the intermission (`pacman.asm:1616` `ld a,(#4e13)`, `:1617` `cp #14`). RED-anchor the exact
  act→level cadence (act 1 after board 2, act 2 after board 5, act 3 after boards 9/13/17) to
  the source — cite it, don't assume (Decision C).
- **Request pm2 music (#02).** Fire the looping intermission music, cited
  `pacman.asm:1613` (`ld a,#02`), `:1615-1616` (`ld (#4ecc),a / ld (#4edc),a`), grounded in
  pm2's `claims/sound.json #02`. Consumed, not re-implemented.
- **Pure-first.** No animation here — the phase + trigger only (purity.test.ts green); the
  scripted actors are pm6-2/pm6-3. Every constant → `citations.test.ts`.
