## Problem
Raised by Dev and confirmed by the Reviewer on ad1-2 (2026-08-01), which opted battlezone into the carousel. Every guard around the showcase asserts MEMBERSHIP or WIRING; none asserts the framed game is actually alive. A game could regress to a black, frozen, or exception-throwing pane and the entire suite stays green. THE CENSUS, all measured on ad1-2: src/host/registry.test.ts pins exactly who is in the carousel (['tempest','battlezone','centipede']) and nothing about what they draw; lobby/tests/main.test.ts:156 derives its expectation from LISTED_GAMES.filter(g => g.showcase) and asserts the launch link's href, not the frame's contents; lobby/src/shell/showcase.ts's own failure handling keys on LOAD_TIMEOUT_MS (8 s) of silence before the load event, and docs/ops/hosting.md states plainly that "A game serving a broken build still fires load and looks healthy from the lobby"; and `just check-showcase` does NOT close this either — hosting.md:296-330 says "What that recipe measures is the HTTP status of /<id>/, and nothing else", so a 200 serving a broken bundle passes it. WHAT ad1-2 DID BY HAND, and this is the method to automate rather than re-invent: serve the tree, let the carousel reach the game, then reach into the same-origin iframe's canvas and sample lit-pixel counts over time — ten samples at 250 ms intervals gave ten DISTINCT counts in the upper band and nine of ten in the lower, which a static text page cannot produce. That evidence lived in one session and dies with it. WHY THIS IS NOT uf1-19: uf1-19 pins lobby TEST-APPARATUS guards (accessible-name.ts helpers, slideFor's ALPHA-only cases) — DOM fixtures in jsdom. This finding is about the framed GAME rendering, which jsdom cannot observe at all; the two do not overlap and routing this into uf1-19 would quietly drop it. SCOPE NOTE: this is epic-wide and rising — ad1-1, ad1-3, ad1-4 and ad1-5 each add another game to the carousel, so the unguarded surface grows with every story in ad1. Decide explicitly whether the check belongs in CI (needs a real browser — the repo has no such harness today, which is the main cost) or as a documented manual gate in docs/ops/hosting.md; do not assume the first without pricing it.
