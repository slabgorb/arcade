## Problem
TWO false clauses in one claim, both PRE-EXISTING and both shipping GREEN. plugins/joust/docs/rom-study/claims/audio.json, claim JT51-009. (1) It says SNPCR1 'is bound to the knight through P1DEC's decision block rather than a direct load (JOUSTRV4.SRC:5544)'. :5544 is G1DEC's sound row; P1DEC's is :5552. The claim pairs the right LABEL with the wrong LINE — the identical misattribution jt5-6 corrected in audio-manifest.ts and left uncorrected in the dossier. (2) It says 'P2 carries its own table SNPCR2 (:8119), which jt5-1 collapses onto this one'. jt5-6 UN-collapsed that: player2Materialise now exists in CUE_SOURCES and cites SNPCR2 :8119 itself, so the clause describes a port that no longer exists. WHY IT SHIPS GREEN: the citation gate verifies a claim's source (file, line, verbatim) and NEVER reads the claim prose — tools/audit/check-citations.mjs:36-42 states that limit about itself. The source here is byte-valid, so nothing fails. SUGGESTED FIX, verified against the ROM during jt5-23: replace "P1DEC's decision block rather than a direct load (JOUSTRV4.SRC:5544)" with "a decision-block binding rather than a direct load (P1DEC :5552)", and delete the 'which jt5-1 collapses onto this one' clause. Leave the source block untouched. CONTEXT NOW AVAILABLE: jt5-23 recorded what the G and P families ARE (attract demo vs real play, selected by GOVER at JOUSTRV4.SRC:1025-1029) in audio-manifest.ts — read that record first; it explains why a G-row citation under a P-label is wrong rather than merely imprecise.
