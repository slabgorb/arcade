## Problem
Two parts of the same overlay. (A) DESIGN — add the skeuomorphic periscope bezel that frames the play area: the cabinet's viewport is seen through a periscope mask, and the clone currently renders edge-to-edge with no framing. This is cabinet artwork, NOT in the ROM or MAME (MAME models only the color multiply, layout/bzone.lay), so it is eyeball/reference-driven from real-cabinet footage (references/footage) — a shell-only HUD-space overlay, drawn over the world and under the score/radar so the HUD stays legible, same layering as drawCrackedGlass. (B) FIDELITY — pin the red/green color split to MAME's authoritative geometry: layout/bzone.lay defines the physical color overlay as RED from top=0 to bottom=0.2 (rgb 1.0,0.125,0.125) and GREEN from 0.2 to 1.0 (rgb 0.125,1.0,0.125), blended "multiply". Our HUD already uses HUD_RED for the score/ radar band and GLOW_GREEN for the field; verify the red band's lower edge sits at the 0.2 split and the field is green below it, and document any deliberate deviation. Note bzone.cpp:855 records a blue-overlay variant — out of scope, green cabinet is canonical.
