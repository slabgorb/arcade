## Problem
Found by sw8-21's review (Dev finding, ROM-verified). The TRENCH is the third and last member of the death-gate family that sw8-13 (space, PHESP1) and sw8-21 (surface, PHEGD) closed, and it still has the bug.

ROM: PHEBS 'VIEW BASE TRENCH' (WSMAIN.MAC:1839) carries the identical guard — 'LDA S.GAS / LBMI PHIB0D ;J EXIT WHEN PLAYER DIES' at :1842-1843 — and it sits ABOVE that phase's entire PH.TIM cue walk, which includes 'JSR PMRRP ;THEN DO REBEL REPEAT THEME' (:1865) and 'JSR SPKTRU ;LUKE, TRUST ME' (:1874). So on the cabinet a last-shield fall silences the trench's voice and theme cues for that frame.

OURS: stepTrench (sim.ts:1210) pushes the TRENCH_VOICE_CUES lines at sim.ts:1247-1252 with no lives gate, and the phase's FIRST shield resolution is loseShield at :1421 — 171 lines later. Same class and same shape as sw8-21: the cue is decided before the frame's death is known.

NOT a copy of sw8-21's fix. The trench has TWO shield resolutions — the gun/obstacle hit at :1421 and the exhaust-port hit at :1571 — so this story needs a ruling on which cues each one gates, rather than a single relocation. sw8-21's fix (move the push below the resolution, gate on the post-hit lives) is the template for the shape, not the placement.

Test template: the sw8-21 block in tests/core/surface-traversal-end.test.ts and the sw8-13 block in tests/core/space-music-milestones.test.ts. Note the trench cue crossing is TIMER-driven (trenchTimer, ROM word_4B0E) — unlike the surface's SPEED-driven crossing — so the fixture differs from sw8-21's again.

Lesson carried forward from sw8-21's three review rounds: do NOT write line-number or count censuses into the test comments. Anchor claims on mechanism names (the function, the const) and on the invariant that loseShield is where damage becomes death. sw8-21 was rejected twice for exactly that.

Reachable only when a cue crossing and the last shield's fall share one frame.
