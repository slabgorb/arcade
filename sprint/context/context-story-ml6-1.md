## Acceptance Criteria

Defined by TEA in RED and pinned by `plugins/millipede/tests/sound-rom.test.ts`
against the vendored `reference/original-source/millipede/{MLIRQ,MLDEF}.MAC`.
GREEN ships `plugins/millipede/src/shell/sound-rom.ts` (a pure module — no
fetch/DOM/canvas/clock/entropy) exporting `NCHAN`, `EFFECT_NAMES`, `pokeyOf`,
`MASK`, `shouldTick`, `freqSweep`, `contSweep`, each cited-constant carrying a
`citations.test.ts`-gated claim.

- **AC-1** — the pure `src/shell/sound-rom.ts` seam exists and exports the driver surface.
- **AC-2** — twelve sound slots: `NCHAN` is DECIMAL 11 (the radix trap — MLDEF.MAC:190 under `.RADIX 16`), `EFFECT_NAMES` names CHAN0..CHAN11 in order (MLDEF.MAC:312-323).
- **AC-3** — two-POKEY routing: `pokeyOf` maps slots 0..7 → POKEY #0 (AUDF0/AUDC0 $400/$401) and 8..11 → POKEY #1 (AUDF1/AUDC1 $800/$801), split exactly at 8 (MLIRQ.MAC:57-58).
- **AC-4** — the mask is the tempo: `MASK` is the twelve ROM frame-masks (MLIRQ.MAC:121-123) and `shouldTick(i, intct)` fires on `(MASK[i] & intct) === 0` (MLIRQ.MAC:17-19).
- **AC-5** — the sweep IS the sound: every table-backed slot's `freqSweep`/`contSweep` is non-constant and matches the ROM envelope as a multiset/range (explosion FREQ2/CONT2, centipede-feet rest pulse CONT1, player-explosion FREQ10, constant-volume shot FREQ6 over CONT 0x68 — MLIRQ.MAC:148-239).
- **AC-6** — externally-stuffed slots (dragonfly 4, bee 7: FREQ pointer 0) synthesize no frequency sweep — `freqSweep` empty (MLIRQ.MAC:100-111,128,131).
- **AC-7** — the driver loop moves (a played sweep emits a changing pitch on the right POKEY), the module passes the ml1-1 purity scan, and no MAME/`.cpp` code crosses the GPL seam.

**Scope note:** the play DIRECTION of each sweep (the NY reverse-read, MLIRQ.MAC:38-40,106-107) is intentionally NOT asserted — see the Delivery Finding. Binding sweeps to `@shared/synth`, the gesture gate, event mapping, and attract-mute are ml6-2.
