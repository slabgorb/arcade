## Problem
Filed by jt9-14 (RED phase) after settling that story's "read the ROM's collision comparison" obligation.

FINDING. The port's narrowPhase (joust.ts:177) models the ROM's BPCOL span-mask test (JOUSTRV4.SRC:7043) but DROPS its COLDX term. BPCOL subtracts COLDX (= ptero.posX - player.posX, the screen-X separation of the two sprites) from the column comparison at :7047/:7051/:7062 etc., so its span overlap is computed in SCREEN space. The port instead compares COFF-unbiased SPRITE-LOCAL columns with the two origins superimposed (COLDX = 0), leaning entirely on broadPhase (a coarse 16px AABB) to carry X. So narrowPhase is X-blind: two sprites up to 15px apart in X whose masks overlap when superimposed register a hit the ROM would reject once COLDX shifts the spans apart.

SCOPE. This is a PRE-EXISTING divergence dating to jt2-3, inherited unexamined. It affects ALL THREE narrowPhase consumers equally — the joust pass, the jt8-7 egg catch, and (as of jt9-14) the player-vs-ptero pass. jt9-14 deliberately wired the existing (COLDX-less) narrowPhase into the third pass to make it CONSISTENT with the other two and match the ROM's BPCOL-gates-OSTHIT structure (:4944-4945); correcting the COLDX omission is this story's job and fixes all three at once.

THE FIX. Fold COLDX into narrowPhase's column compare (shift spanB by the screen-X delta before intervalsOverlap), and thread the two entities' screen X into every call site. Expect the mask bands measured in jt9-14 (dx=0 only) to widen/shift for dx!=0; re-baseline jt9-14's dx=0 fixtures (COLDX-invariant, should be untouched) and any dx!=0 sibling (audio-emission/menagerie/jt4-4/jt5-16 stage at dx=4). Verify against BPCOL byte-for-byte.
